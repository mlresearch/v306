title: 'SGERA: Stein-Guided ECG-Report Alignment for ECG Representation Learning'
openreview: iEtOxzAs51
abstract: 'Electrocardiogram (ECG) representation learning via ECG-report alignment
  is often hindered by the inherent structural and statistical divergence between
  signals and natural language. Existing methods struggle to bridge this gap with
  simple contrastive objectives, but struggle with distribution dependencies between
  heterogeneous features. To address this, we propose <b>SGERA</b> (<b>S</b>tein-<b>G</b>uided
  <b>E</b>CG-<b>R</b>eport <b>A</b>lignment), which leverages the unique properties
  of Stein kernels to provide a more rigorous geometric alignment in the latent space:
  <b>instance-level</b> alignment via a Stein-RBF kernel enforces pairwise consistency
  between ECG and report embeddings and <b>distribution-level</b> alignment via a
  Stein-Score kernel captures higher-order interactions for global alignment. Furthermore,
  we introduce an ECG-Report matching task with a Hard Sample Mining strategy to refine
  discriminative boundaries. Experiments across three public datasets demonstrate
  that SGERA significantly outperforms state-of-the-art SSL methods in zero-shot classification,
  linear probing, and transfer learning, proving the superiority of Stein-guided alignment
  in handling complex medical modalities. Code is available at supplementary materials.'
