title: End-to-End Autoregressive Image Generation with 1D Semantic Tokenizer
openreview: fSnxuRhWoC
abstract: Autoregressive image modeling relies on visual tokenizers to compress images
  into compact latent representations. We design an end-to-end training pipeline that
  jointly optimizes reconstruction and generation, enabling direct supervision from
  generation results to the tokenizer. This contrasts with prior two-stage approaches
  that train tokenizers and generative models separately. We further investigate leveraging
  vision foundation models to improve 1D tokenizers for autoregressive modeling. Our
  autoregressive generative model achieves strong empirical results, including a state-of-the-art
  FID score of 1.48 without guidance on ImageNet 256$\times$256 generation.
