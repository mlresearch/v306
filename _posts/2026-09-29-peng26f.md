abstract: 'Value factorization eases non-stationarity in MARL, but its static coordination
  assumptions hinder generalization on long-horizon tasks with shifting dependencies.
  Prior VQ-VAE methods abstract trajectories yet miss time-varying inter-agent dependencies.
  We present TACTIC, a CTDE framework with three components: (i) VQ-VAE-based trajectory
  abstraction that learns discrete task-semantic classes; (ii) semantic-conditioned
  sparse coordination graphs that adapt dependencies by pruning edges according to
  variance-based pairwise payoff sensitivity; and (iii) a pretrained, frozen trajectory-class
  predictor that conditions local policies while decoupling task recognition from
  control. On SMAC and SUMO, TACTIC shows strong overall competitiveness and adaptive
  coordination under sparse rewards and dynamic task structures.'
