title: 'Reasoning Theater: Disentangling Model Beliefs from Chain-of-Thought'
openreview: 1lyagkzogH
abstract: 'We provide evidence of <em>performative</em> chain-of-thought (CoT) in
  reasoning models, where a model becomes strongly confident in its final answer,
  but continues generating tokens without revealing its internal belief. Our analysis
  compares activation probing, early forced answering, and a CoT monitor across two
  large models (DeepSeek-R1 671B & GPT-OSS 120B) and find task difficulty-specific
  differences: The model’s final answer is decodable from activations far earlier
  in CoT than a monitor is able to say, especially for easy recall-based MMLU questions.
  We contrast this with genuine reasoning in difficult multihop GPQA-Diamond questions.
  Despite this, <em>inflection points</em> (e.g., backtracking, ‘aha’ moments) occur
  almost exclusively in responses where probes show large belief shifts, suggesting
  these behaviors track genuine uncertainty rather than learned “reasoning theater."
  Finally, probe-guided early exit reduces tokens by up to 80% on MMLU and 30% on
  GPQA-Diamond with similar accuracy, positioning attention probing as an efficient
  tool for detecting performative reasoning and enabling adaptive computation.'
