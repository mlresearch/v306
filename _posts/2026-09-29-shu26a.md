abstract: Size transfer scales Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) by applying models
  trained on sampled subgraphs to larger target graphs. However, existing theoretical
  guarantees are typically confined to dense graphs or restricted sparsity regimes,
  failing to cover the arbitrary sparsity of real-world networks. To bridge this gap,
  we introduce the Generalized Graphon Convolutional Network (GWCN) based on the generalized
  graphon theory. Unlike the classical graphon limit which vanishes in sparse settings,
  GWCN employs stretching to construct a non-trivial limit that preserves topological
  structure. We derive an explicit transfer error bound that decomposes into size-dependent
  and density-dependent components, providing a unified guarantee across arbitrary
  sparsity levels. Empirical results on real-world networks corroborate our findings,
  demonstrating that transfer error vanishes as graph size increases and edge density
  decreases.
