title: 'STRIDE: Post-Training LLMs to Reason and Refine Bio-Sequences via Edit Trajectories'
openreview: g9J8AyE0xy
abstract: 'Discrete biological sequence optimization often requires goal-directed,
  parser-valid edits to an existing protein or molecule. Diffusion models support
  iterative refinement but do not expose a controllable discrete-edit interface, while
  autoregressive LLMs can be myopic when planning constrained edits over multiple
  steps. We introduce <em>STRIDE</em> (Sequence Trajectory Refinement via Iterative
  Discrete Editing), a post-training framework that trains an LLM to emit executable
  INSERT/DELETE/REPLACE trajectories for variable-length refinement. <em>STRIDE</em>
  first learns Levenshtein-aligned shortest-edit demonstrations, then uses supervised
  fine-tuning and group-based policy optimization to align trajectories with task
  rewards while preserving coherent editing. On an oracle-based full-action protein
  stress test, <em>STRIDE</em> raises success over Vanilla SFT from 42% to 89% and
  novelty among unique improvements from 47% to 97%. On instruction-conditioned molecular
  editing, the GSPO-aligned variant improves strict success, controllability, and
  SMILES validity over the SFT-only <em>STRIDE</em> model (code: https://github.com/daiheng-zhang/STRIDE).'
