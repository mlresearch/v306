abstract: Autonomous web agents are increasingly deployed for long-horizon tasks,
  yet their ability to adhere to real-world policies remains critically underexplored
  compared to standard safety objectives. To address this gap, we introduce PolicyGuardBench,
  a benchmark of 60k policy-trajectory pairs designed to evaluate compliance through
  both full-trajectory and novel prefix-based violation detection tasks. Using this
  dataset, we train PolicyGuard, a lightweight guardrail model that achieves strong
  detection accuracy while maintaining high inference efficiency. Notably, our model
  demonstrates robust generalization capabilities, preserving high performance even
  on unseen domains. These contributions establish a comprehensive framework for studying
  policy compliance, showing that accurate and generalizable guardrails are feasible
  at small scales.
