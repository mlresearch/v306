abstract: Understanding the internal mechanisms of deep neural networks remains a
  significant challenge, particularly in elucidating how generic visual concepts emerge
  within latent spaces. In this work, we propose SAEs-BrainMap, a novel framework
  that utilizes human brain activation patterns from the ventral visual pathway as
  objective probes to guide the identification of features decomposed by Sparse Autoencoders
  (SAEs). Our quantitative and qualitative empirical results demonstrate a robust
  representational alignment between sparse model features and biological Regions
  of Interests (ROIs), confirming the feasibility of utilizing brain signals to characterize
  model functionality. By leveraging this alignment, we trace the hierarchical trajectory
  of generic concepts cross layers and utilize the brain’s hierarchical structure
  to visualize the model’s global processing flow, providing novel insights into model
  interpretability. Our code is available at this repository.
