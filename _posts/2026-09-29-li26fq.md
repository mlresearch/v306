abstract: While edge GPUs are increasingly used for latency-critical DNN tasks, limited
  resources often fail to meet strict real-time (RT) requirements under concurrent
  workloads. Existing preemption and early-exit mechanisms often underutilize GPU
  resources through single-task queuing and sacrifice excessive accuracy during task
  bursts. To address this, we propose RTInfer, a novel system that enables concurrent
  RT task execution while balancing throughput and accuracy. RTInfer integrates an
  accuracy-calibrated lightweight variant co-optimization to generate efficient models,
  a memory-layout-aware scheduler to mitigate fragmentation during preemption, and
  an on-demand loading strategy to minimize host-to-GPU latency. Extensive evaluations
  demonstrate that RTInfer outperforms state-of-the-art methods by reducing average
  deadline miss rate (DMR) from 32.8% to 0% and improving accuracy by up to 56.5%.
