title: 'RN-D: Discretized Categorical Actors for On-Policy Reinforcement Learning'
openreview: saZwSnt3r9
abstract: On-policy Reinforcement Learning (RL) remains a dominant paradigm for continuous
  control, yet standard implementations rely on Gaussian actors and relatively shallow
  MLP policies, often leading to brittle optimization when gradients are noisy, and
  policy updates must be conservative. In this paper, we revisit actor policy representation
  as a first-class design choice for on-policy RL. We study discretized categorical
  actors, which represent each action dimension as a distribution over discrete bins
  and induce a policy objective analogous to classification cross-entropy loss. Building
  on architectural advances from supervised learning, we further pair discretized
  categorical actors with regularized networks, yielding RN-D. Across diverse continuous-control
  benchmarks, we show that simply replacing the standard Gaussian actor with our proposed
  actor substantially improves performance, achieving state-of-the-art results within
  on-policy RL. We release our code at https://github.com/alwaysbyx/RND-RL.
