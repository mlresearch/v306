title: 'BYORn: Bootstrap Your Own Responses to Defend Large Vision-Language Models
  Against Backdoor Attacks'
openreview: iyO15Uqy75
abstract: Supervised fine-tuning is the predominant approach for adapting autoregressive
  vision–language models to downstream tasks. Recent work has shown that this paradigm
  is highly vulnerable to backdoor attacks, and that existing defenses are ineffective
  in open-ended generation settings. In response, we propose BYORn, a backdoor-robust
  fine-tuning framework motivated by the observation that poisoned target responses
  are often semantically implausible given the corresponding image–text inputs and
  a pretrained model. BYORn identifies such misaligned responses and dynamically replaces
  them with alternative responses generated by the model, thereby breaking the correlation
  between triggers and target outputs. The resulting objective gradient corresponds
  to the gradient of the empirical estimate of the population risk upper bound over
  the clean data distribution. Empirically, BYORn consistently improves robustness
  to backdoor attacks while preserving clean-task performance, establishing a new
  trade-off frontier between generalization and attack success rate. Finally, we demonstrate
  that BYORn remains effective against adaptive attacks specifically designed to circumvent
  the proposed defense.
