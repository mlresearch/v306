abstract: Targeted instruction tuning requires selecting pertinent samples from massive
  mixed <em>candidate datasets</em> guided by a small <em>reference dataset</em> reflecting
  the desired capability, yet efficiently identifying high-quality data amidst noise
  remains challenging. To address this, we propose <b>TarGATE</b> (<b>Tar</b>get-aware
  <b>GATE</b>s, a simple yet effective data selection framework that leverages the
  model’s inherent data understanding. TarGATE computes a token-level Information
  Retention Ratio (<b>IRR</b>) to scale the output of the feed-forward network, where
  the instance-level average IRR serves as a quantitative metric for data quality.
  To align gates’ preferences with the target task, we employ a joint optimization
  strategy utilizing the reference set and a subset of candidate data, which encourages
  the gates to assign higher IRRs to reference-aligned data while suppressing low-quality
  samples. Extensive experiments across noisy and real-world scenarios demonstrate
  that TarGATE outperforms related baselines. Furthermore, TarGATE exhibits superior
  computational efficiency and strong cross-model transferability, enabling smaller
  selector to effectively curate high-quality fine-tuning data for larger foundation
  models. The code is available at here.
