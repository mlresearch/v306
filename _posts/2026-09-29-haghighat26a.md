abstract: Building on recent formalizations of root cause analysis for rare events
  (“outliers”) in structural equation models, we propose a formal definition of a
  causal pathway and discuss its testable implications. We identify conditions under
  which these implications depend only on a causal abstraction defined by the pathway
  of rare events, rather than on the full causal graph of the underlying system. Accordingly,
  we introduce an abstraction of causal structure to pathways of rare events that
  bridges simple verbal causal explanations and detailed causal modeling.
