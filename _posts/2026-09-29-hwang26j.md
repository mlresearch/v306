abstract: 'Modern deep learning for asset allocation typically separates forecasting
  from optimization. We argue this creates a fundamental mismatch where minimizing
  prediction errors fails to yield robust portfolios. We propose the Signature Informed
  Transformer to address this by unifying feature extraction and decision making into
  a single policy. Our model employs path signatures to encode complex path dependencies
  and introduces a specialized attention mechanism that targets geometric asset relationships.
  By directly minimizing the Conditional Value at Risk we ensure the training objective
  aligns with financial goals. We prove that our attention module rigorously amplifies
  signature derived signals. Experiments across diverse equity universes show our
  approach significantly outperforms both traditional strategies and advanced forecasting
  baselines. The code is available at: https://github.com/Yoontae6719/Signature-Informed-Transformer-For-Asset-Allocation'
