title: Flexible Kernels for Protein Property Prediction

abstract: Despite its importance to applications in protein design, predicting protein
  properties like binding affinity and thermostability from sparse experimental data
  remains a significant challenge. Accordingly, we introduce a class of sequence kernels
  that exploit evolutionary substitution matrices as well as local linearity and demonstrate
  that the resulting Gaussian processes provide data-efficient models of protein property
  landscapes, frequently outperforming alternatives that rely on foundation model
  embeddings. Furthermore–by learning what are in effect structure-aware substitution
  matrices–we show that our kernels can readily incorporate structural information
  from foundation models. We demonstrate that these structure-conditioned kernels
  are well suited to multi-task learning across multiple protein property landscapes
  and can decisively outperform local supervised learning methods.
