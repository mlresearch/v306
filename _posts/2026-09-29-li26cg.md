abstract: Incomplete multi-view multi-label classification addresses scenarios where
  views and labels are partially missing. While existing methods treat labels solely
  as supervision signals, they overlook the semantic structure inherent in partial
  annotations. We propose Label-Guided Representation Learning (LGRL) that systematically
  exploits label semantics as structural priors throughout learning. Our framework
  constructs a semantic-informed mixture prior via learnable category prototypes to
  guide representation extraction, and introduces category-specific conditional posteriors
  where prototypes act as Bayesian experts for multi-view fusion. We further derive
  a principled label-driven information bottleneck objective balancing reconstruction
  sufficiency with cross-view consistency, enabling category-conditional reasoning.
  Extensive experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of LGRL across benchmark
  datasets as well as real-world applications in sports analytics and medical imaging.
