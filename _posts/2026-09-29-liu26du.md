  Text-guided Image-to-Video Generation'

abstract: Text-guided image-to-video generation has made substantial progress, yet
  it still struggles to execute text-specified edits that require substantial changes
  to a reference image (e.g., object addition, deletion, or modification). Empirically,
  our analysis reveals that this stems from <b>visual dominance</b>, where the reference
  image causes severe attention dispersion, inhibiting the model’s ability to incorporate
  new semantic information. To address this, we propose <b>AlignVid</b>, a training-free
  intervention that re-calibrates the model’s internal attention distribution. Drawing
  on an energy-based perspective of attention, AlignVid employs Attention Scaling
  Modulation (<b>ASM</b>) to reduce attention entropy and concentrate focus on semantic
  tokens, alongside Guidance Scheduling (<b>GS</b>) to maintain generation stability.
  To rigorously assess this capability, we present <b>OmitI2V</b>, a comprehensive
  benchmark for evaluating prompt adherence across object addition, deletion, and
  modification. Extensive experiments demonstrate that AlignVid effectively enhances
  semantic fidelity with negligible computational overhead.
