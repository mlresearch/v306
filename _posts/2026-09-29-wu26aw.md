abstract: 'We introduce Genome-Factory, the first integrated Python library for tuning,
  deploying, and interpreting genomic foundation models. Our core contribution is
  to simplify and unify the workflow for genomic model development: data collection,
  model tuning, inference, benchmarking, and interpretability. For data collection,
  Genome-Factory offers an automated pipeline to download genomic sequences and preprocess
  them. For model tuning, Genome-Factory supports both full and parameter-efficient
  fine-tuning across diverse genomic models. For inference, Genome-Factory enables
  both embedding extraction and DNA sequence generation. For benchmarking, we include
  two existing benchmarks and provide a flexible interface to incorporate additional
  benchmarks. For interpretability, Genome-Factory introduces an open-source biological
  interpreter based on a sparse auto-encoder. We validate the utility of Genome-Factory
  across three dimensions: (i) Compatibility with diverse models and fine-tuning methods;
  (ii) Benchmarking downstream performance using two open-source benchmarks; (iii)
  Biological interpretation of learned representations with DNABERT-2. These results
  highlight its practical value for real-world genomic analysis. GitHub: https://github.com/WeiminWu2000/Genome_Factory.'
