title: 'ParisKV: Fast and Drift-Robust KV-Cache Retrieval for Long-Context LLMs'
openreview: wxD4wTYQXt
abstract: 'KV-cache retrieval is essential for long-context LLM inference, yet existing
  methods struggle with distribution drift and high latency at scale. We introduce
  <b>ParisKV</b>, a drift-robust, GPU-native KV-cache retrieval framework based on
  collision-based candidate selection, followed by a quantized inner-product reranking
  estimator. For million-token contexts, ParisKV supports CPU-offloaded KV caches
  via Unified Virtual Addressing (UVA), enabling on-demand top-$k$ fetching with minimal
  overhead. ParisKV matches or outperforms full attention quality on both <b>long-input</b>
  and <b>long-generation</b> benchmarks. It achieves state-of-the-art long-context
  decoding efficiency: it matches or exceeds full-attention speed even at batch size
  1 for long contexts, delivers up to <b>2.8$\times$</b> higher throughput within
  full attention’s runnable range, and scales to <b>million-token</b> contexts where
  full attention runs out of memory. At million-token scale, ParisKV reduces decode
  latency by <b>17$\times$</b> and <b>44$\times$</b> compared to MagicPIG and PQCache,
  respectively—two state-of-the-art KV-cache top-$k$ retrieval baselines; code is
  available at https://github.com/amy-77/ParisKV/tree/main.'
