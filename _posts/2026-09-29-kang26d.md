abstract: 'We present an amortized framework for real-time visual attribution streaming
  in multimodal thinking models. When these models generate code from a screenshot
  or solve math problems from images, their long reasoning traces should be grounded
  in visual evidence. However, verifying this reliance is challenging: faithful causal
  methods require costly repeated backward passes or perturbations, while raw attention
  maps offer instant access, they lack causal validity. To resolve this, we introduce
  an amortized approach that learns to estimate the causal effects of semantic regions
  directly from the rich signals encoded in attention features. Across five diverse
  benchmarks and four thinking models, our approach achieves faithfulness comparable
  to exhaustive causal methods while enabling visual attribution streaming, where
  users observe grounding evidence as the model reasons, not after. Our results demonstrate
  that real-time, faithful attribution in multimodal thinking models is achievable
  through lightweight learning, not brute-force computation.'
