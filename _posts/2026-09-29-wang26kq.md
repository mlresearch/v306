title: Dual-branch Robust Unlearnable Examples
openreview: mHhdnUufwR
abstract: Unlearnable examples (UEs) aim to compromise model training by injecting
  imperceptible perturbations to clean samples. However, existing UE schemes exhibit
  limited robustness against advanced defenses due to their heuristic design or narrowly
  scoped domain perturbations. To address this, we propose DUNE, a Dual-branch UNlearnable
  Ensemble perturbation optimization approach. Specifically, DUNE separately optimizes
  perturbations in the spatial and color domains to establish the mapping between
  perturbations and shift-induced labels. This design extends the perturbation domain
  to increase noise intensity for improving robustness and drives the models to learn
  perturbation-oriented features with degraded generalization, thereby achieving unlearnability.
  To strengthen DUNE’s performance, we further propose an unlearnability-enhancing
  ensemble strategy that aggregates diverse pre-trained models during the dual-branch
  optimization. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets CIFAR-10 and ImageNet
  verify that DUNE’s robustness outperforms 12 SOTA UE schemes under 7 mainstream
  defenses, yielding a lower average test accuracy of 14.95% to 50.82%.
