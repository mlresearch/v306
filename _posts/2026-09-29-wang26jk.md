abstract: We study a multi-factor block model for variable clustering and connect
  it to regularized subspace clustering through a distributionally robust version
  of nodewise regression. To solve the latter problem, we derive a convex relaxation,

  develop an ADMM algorithm for efficient implementation. We validate our method in
  extensive numerical studies and demonstrate its superior performance.
