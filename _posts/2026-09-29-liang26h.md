abstract: Scene graphs provide structured abstractions for scene understanding, yet
  they often overfit to spurious correlations, severely hindering out-of-distribution
  generalization. To address this limitation, we propose CURVE, a causality-inspired
  framework that integrates variational uncertainty modeling with uncertainty-guided
  structural regularization to suppress high-variance, environment-specific relations.
  Specifically, we apply prototype-conditioned debiasing to disentangle invariant
  interaction dynamics from environment-dependent variations, promoting a sparse and
  domain-stable topology. Empirically, we evaluate CURVE in zero-shot transfer and
  low-data sim-to-real adaptation, verifying its ability to learn domain-stable sparse
  topologies and provide reliable uncertainty estimates to support risk prediction
  under distribution shifts.
