abstract: 'Dataset bias often leads deep learning models to exploit spurious correlations
  instead of task-relevant signals. We introduce the Standard Anti-Causal Model (SAM),
  a unifying causal framework that characterizes bias mechanisms and yields a conditional
  independence criterion for causal stability. Building on this theory, we propose
  DISCO$_m$ and sDISCO, efficient and scalable estimators of conditional distance
  correlation that enable independence regularization in gradient-based models. Across
  six diverse datasets, our methods consistently outperform or are competitive in
  existing observed bias mitigation approaches, while requiring fewer hyperparameters
  and scaling seamlessly to multi-bias scenarios. This work bridges causal theory
  and practical deep learning, providing both a principled foundation and effective
  tools for robust prediction. Source Code: https://github.com/yakamoz5/DISCO.'
