title: Semantic Granularity Navigation in Image Editing

abstract: Despite the generative capabilities of diffusion and flow models, real-image
  editing remains constrained by a persistent trade-off between semantic editability
  and structural fidelity. We trace a primary cause of this limitation to the implicit
  coupling of edit progress with model scale in existing paradigms. Under this coupling,
  stronger edits typically require visiting noisier states, which spends computation
  on destabilizing layout before the semantic change is well localized. We introduce
  NaviEdit, a training-free inference-time controller that decouples edit progress
  from model scale traversal through a strict self-consistency contract. NaviEdit
  operates at the rollout level and leaves the underlying pretrained model unchanged.
  It treats scale as a control input and reallocates a fixed step budget toward semantically
  responsive intermediate scales instead of destructive high-noise regimes. Experiments
  show positive average gains across compatible editors and flow backbones, supporting
  decoupling as a portable inference-time control principle.
