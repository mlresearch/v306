title: 'SURGE: Approximation and Training Free Particle Filter for Diffusion Surrogate'
openreview: JRsJLPr85W
abstract: Data assimilation (DA) tackles the sequential estimation of a dynamical
  system’s latent state from noisy, partial observations. In this paper, we study
  DA in a setting where the system dynamics are represented by a pretrained diffusion
  model used as a surrogate forecaster. We focus on how to integrate incoming observations
  into the diffusion surrogate’s predictions to support continuous state correction
  and progressively refining the estimated trajectory over time. After receiving noisy
  observations, the diffusion model is guided using the observation likelihood to
  steer the generation process toward observation-consistent states. However, such
  guidance does not guarantee sampling from the true posterior. Motivated by particle
  filtering methods, we represent the posterior distribution using an ensemble of
  particles. We perform Sequential Monte Carlo over diffusion trajectories, working
  with their path measure. We compute importance weights for generated particles and
  resample to focus on trajectories consistent with the observations. This procedure
  corrects the generation dynamics, drives the particle approximation toward the desired
  posterior and leads to an approximation-free particle filtering method that rigorously
  fuses observational data with diffusion model simulations.
