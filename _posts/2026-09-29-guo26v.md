title: Learning to Route Languages for Multilingual Policy Optimization
openreview: 13lMChKB5u
abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are trained on heterogeneous multilingual corpora,
  yet existing policy optimization methods often implicitly restrict each training
  question to a single response language or rely on a fixed dominant language for
  supervision. We propose language-routed policy optimization (LRPO), an online reinforcement
  learning framework that treats language as a selectable variable. LRPO elicits multilingual
  rollouts for each training question and integrates their relative quality into preference-based
  policy updates, increasing the diversity and informativeness of training signals
  under the fixed rollout budget. To adaptively determine which languages to explore
  during reinforcement learning, we introduce a trainable language router formulated
  as a multi-armed bandit, balancing exploration of underutilized languages with exploitation
  of more informative ones. Extensive experiments show that LRPO consistently improves
  multilingual performance, demonstrating that adaptive language routing enables effective
  cross-lingual knowledge exploitation for training. We release all the resources
  at https://github.com/Guochry/LRPO.
