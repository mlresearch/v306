abstract: 'SAM3D enables scalable, open-world 3D reconstruction from complex scenes,
  yet its deployment is hindered by prohibitive inference latency. In this work, we
  conduct the <b>first systematic investigation</b> into its inference dynamics, revealing
  that generic acceleration strategies are brittle in this context. We demonstrate
  that these failures stem from neglecting the pipeline’s inherent multi-level <b>heterogeneity</b>:
  the kinematic distinctiveness between shape and layout, the intrinsic sparsity of
  texture refinement, and the spectral variance across geometries. To address this,
  we present <b>Fast-SAM3D</b>, a training-free framework that dynamically aligns
  computation with instantaneous generation complexity. Our approach integrates three
  heterogeneity-aware mechanisms: (1) <em>Modality-Aware Step Caching</em> to decouple
  structural evolution from sensitive layout updates; (2) <em>Joint Spatiotemporal
  Token Carving</em> to concentrate refinement on high-entropy regions; and (3) <em>Spectral-Aware
  Token Aggregation</em> to adapt decoding resolution. Extensive experiments demonstrate
  that Fast-SAM3D delivers up to <b>2.67$\times$</b> end-to-end speedup with negligible
  fidelity loss, establishing a new Pareto frontier for efficient single-view 3D generation.'
