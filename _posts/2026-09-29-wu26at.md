abstract: Despite the advanced capabilities of Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs),
  they frequently suffer from object hallucination. One reason is that visual features
  and pretrained textual representations often become intertwined in the deeper network
  layers. To address this, we propose REVIS, a training-free framework designed to
  explicitly re-activate this suppressed visual information. Rooted in latent space
  geometry, REVIS extracts the pure visual information vector via orthogonal projection
  and employs a calibrated strategy to perform sparse intervention only at the precise
  depth where suppression occurs. This surgical approach effectively restores visual
  information with minimal computational cost. Empirical evaluations on standard benchmarks
  demonstrate that REVIS reduces object hallucination rates by approximately 19% compared
  to state-of-the-art baselines, while preserving general reasoning capabilities.
