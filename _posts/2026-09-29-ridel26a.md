title: Improved Bounds for Reward-Agnostic and Reward-Free Exploration
openreview: dmTyOwKAmZ
abstract: We study <em>reward-free</em> and <em>reward-agnostic</em> exploration in
  episodic finite-horizon Markov decision processes (MDPs), where an agent explores
  an unknown environment without observing external rewards. Reward-free exploration
  aims to enable $\epsilon$-optimal policies for <em>any</em> reward revealed after
  exploration, while reward-agnostic exploration targets $\epsilon$-optimality for
  rewards drawn from a small finite class. In the <em>reward-agnostic setting</em>,
  Li, Yan, Chen, and Fan (2024) achieve minimax sample complexity, but only for restrictively
  small accuracy parameter $\epsilon$. We propose a new algorithm that significantly
  relaxes the requirement on $\epsilon$. Our approach is novel and of technical interest
  by itself. Our algorithm employs an online learning procedure with carefully designed
  rewards to construct an exploration policy, which is used to gather data sufficient
  for accurate dynamics estimation and subsequent computation of an $\epsilon$-optimal
  policy once the reward is revealed. Finally, we establish a tight lower bound for
  <em>reward-free exploration</em>, closing the gap between known upper and lower
  bounds.
