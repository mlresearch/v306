abstract: Existing 3D Visual Question Answering (3D-VQA) methods rely on generative
  outputs that can be ambiguous in decision-making settings. We introduce 3D Scene
  Assertion Verification, a task that verifies natural language assertions in 3D scenes
  with strict binary judgments. We present 3DSAV, a large-scale diagnostic benchmark
  with 22.5k samples across six semantic types. To address this task, we propose DualLPSS,
  which uses dual-stage subspace routing for type-aware cross-modal fusion and scene-guided
  assertion focusing. Experiments show that DualLPSS achieves state-of-the-art performance
  on 3DSAV and handles complex logical assertions better than existing 3D-VQA baselines.
