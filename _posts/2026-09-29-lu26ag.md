title: Context Tuning for In-Context Optimization

abstract: We introduce Context Tuning, a simple and effective method to significantly
  enhance few-shot adaptation of language models (LLMs) without fine-tuning model
  parameters. While prompt-based adaptation techniques have demonstrated the effectiveness
  of lightweight adaptation methods for LLMs, they typically initialize a trainable
  prompt or prefix with irrelevant tokens for the task at hand. In contrast, Context
  Tuning initializes the trainable prompt or prefix with task-specific demonstration
  examples, leveraging the model’s inherent In-Context Learning (ICL) ability to extract
  relevant information for improved few-shot learning performance. Extensive evaluations
  on benchmarks such as CrossFit, UnifiedQA, MMLU, BIG-Bench Hard, and ARC demonstrate
  that Context Tuning outperforms traditional prompt-based adaptation methods and
  achieves competitive accuracy with Test-Time Training at significantly higher training
  efficiency.
