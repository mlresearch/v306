abstract: 'Being probabilistic models, during inference large language models (LLMs)
  display <em>rare events</em>: behaviour that is far from typical but highly significant.
  By definition all rare events are hard to see, but the enormous scale of LLM usage
  means that events completely unobserved during development are likely to become
  prominent in deployment. Here we present an end-to-end framework for the systematic
  analysis of rare events in LLMs. We provide a practical implementation spanning
  theory, efficient generation strategies, probability estimation and error analysis,
  which we illustrate with concrete examples. We outline extensions and applications
  to other models and contexts, highlighting the generality of the concepts and techniques
  presented here.'
