title: 'UniMedVL: Unifying Medical Multimodal Understanding and Generation through
  Observation-Knowledge-Analysis'

abstract: Medical workflows routinely combine reading images with producing visual
  and textual outputs, making both image understanding and generation central to medical
  AI. Most existing systems, however, address these abilities in isolated models,
  losing the shared knowledge that a unified architecture could exploit. To bridge
  this gap, we present UniMedVL, the first unified medical model that seamlessly integrates
  multimodal understanding and generation capabilities within a single model without
  switching weights. We achieve this via a tailored progressive training pipeline
  where understanding and generation mutually reinforce each other. To effectively
  train UniMedVL, we curate UniMedVL-5M, the first large-scale medical dataset comprising
  over 5.6M instances across 8 medical imaging modalities, tailored for multimodal
  input-output tasks in unified medical understanding and generation. Experimental
  results demonstrate that UniMedVL achieves competitive performance on five medical
  understanding benchmarks. Crucially, UniMedVL natively supports diverse interleaved
  generation tasks, e.g., virtual staining, super-resolution, cross-modal synthesis,
  essential for complex medical workflows. Our code and dataset are publicly available.
