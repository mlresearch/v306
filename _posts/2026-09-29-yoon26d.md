title: 'SelfJudge: Faster Speculative Decoding via Self-Supervised Judge Verification'
openreview: 9DAX4oLVhy
abstract: Speculative decoding accelerates LLM inference by verifying candidate tokens
  from a draft model against a larger target model. Recent "judge” decoding boosts
  this process by relaxing verification criteria by accepting draft tokens that may
  exhibit minor discrepancies from target model output, but existing methods are restricted
  by their reliance on human annotations or tasks with verifiable ground truths, limiting
  generalizability across diverse NLP tasks. We propose SelfJudge, which trains judge
  verifiers via self-supervision of the target model. Our method measures semantic
  preservation by assessing whether token-substituted responses preserve the meaning
  of original responses, enabling automatic verifier training across diverse NLP tasks.
  Our experiments show SelfJudge achieves superior inference-accuracy trade-offs than
  judge decoding baselines, offering a broadly applicable solution for faster LLM
  inference.
