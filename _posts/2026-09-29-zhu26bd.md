title: 'RE-TRAC: REcursive TRAjectory Compression for Deep Search Agents'
openreview: wjNTbZKIvC
abstract: LLM-based deep research agents are largely built on the ReAct framework.
  This linear design makes it difficult to revisit earlier states, branch into alternative
  search directions, or maintain global awareness under long contexts, often leading
  to local optima, redundant exploration, and inefficient search. We propose Re-TRAC,
  an agentic framework that performs cross-trajectory exploration by generating a
  structured state representation after each trajectory to summarize evidence, uncertainties,
  failures, and future plans, and conditioning subsequent trajectories on this state
  representation. This enables iterative reflection and globally informed planning,
  reframing research as a progressive process. Empirical results show that Re-TRAC
  consistently outperforms ReAct by 15–20% on BrowseComp with frontier LLMs. For smaller
  models, we introduce Re-TRAC-aware supervised fine-tuning, achieving state-of-the-art
  performance at comparable scales. Notably, Re-TRAC shows a monotonic reduction in
  tool calls and token usage across rounds, indicating progressively targeted exploration
  driven by cross-trajectory reflection rather than redundant search.
