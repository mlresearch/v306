abstract: 'Stochastic Variance Reduced Gradient (SVRG) and its variants aim to speed-up
  training by using gradient corrections. Originally proposed over a decade ago, these
  methods have never been connected to any Bayesian method at a fundamental level.
  Here, we fill this gap and derive surprising new connections of SVRG to a recently
  proposed Bayesian method called ‘posterior correction’. Our main contribution is
  to show that SVRG can be recovered as a special case of posterior-correction over
  isotropic-Gaussian posteriors. Novel extensions of SVRG are automatically obtained
  by using more flexible exponential-family posteriors. We derive two new such extensions
  by using Gaussian families: a Newton-like variant with novel Hessian corrections,
  and an Adam-like extension that scales to large problems. Our work is the first
  to connect SVRG to Bayes and use it to speed-up training.'
