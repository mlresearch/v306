title: 'NonZero: Interaction-Guided Exploration for Multi-Agent Monte Carlo Tree Search'
openreview: Jh6gq9QsFa
abstract: 'Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) scales poorly in cooperative multi-agent
  domains because expansion must consider an exponentially large set of joint actions,
  severely limiting exploration under realistic search budgets. We propose NonZero,
  which keeps multi-agent MCTS tractable by running surrogate-guided selection over
  a low-dimensional nonlinear representation using an interaction-guided proposal
  rule, instead of directly exploring the full joint-action space. Our exploration
  uses an interaction score: single-agent deviations are ranked by predicted gain,
  while two-agent deviations are scored by a mixed-difference measure that reveals
  coordination benefits even when no single agent can improve alone. We formalize
  candidate proposal as a bandit problem over local deviations and derive a proposal
  rule, NonUCT, with a sublinear local-regret guarantee for reaching approximate graph-local
  optima without enumerating the joint-action space. Empirically, NonZero improves
  sample efficiency and final performance on MatGame, SMAC, and SMACv2 relative to
  strong model-based and model-free baselines under matched search budgets.'
