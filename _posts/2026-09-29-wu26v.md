title: 'Infinite-World: Scaling Interactive World Models to 1000-Frame Horizons via
  Pose-Free Hierarchical Memory'
openreview: 27m3BuajLs
abstract: We propose <b>Infinite-World</b>, a robust interactive world model capable
  of maintaining coherent visual memory over <b>1000+ frames</b> in complex real-world
  environments. While existing world models can be efficiently optimized on synthetic
  data with perfect ground-truth, they lack an effective training paradigm for real-world
  videos due to noisy pose estimations and the scarcity of viewpoint revisits. To
  bridge this gap, we first introduce a <b>Hierarchical Pose-free Memory Compressor
  (HPMC)</b> that recursively distills historical latents into a fixed-budget representation.
  By jointly optimizing the compressor with the generative backbone, HPMC enables
  the model to autonomously anchor generations in the distant past with bounded computational
  cost, eliminating the need for explicit geometric priors. Second, we propose an
  <b>Uncertainty-aware Action Abstraction</b> module that discretizes continuous motion
  into a tri-state logic. This strategy maximizes the utilization of raw video data
  while shielding the deterministic action space from being corrupted by noisy trajectories,
  ensuring robust action-response learning. Furthermore, guided by insights from a
  pilot toy study, we employ a <b>Revisit-Dense Finetuning Strategy</b> using a compact,
  30-minute dataset to efficiently activate the model’s long-range loop-closure capabilities.
  Extensive experiments, including objective metrics and user studies, demonstrate
  that Infinite-World achieves superior performance in visual quality, action controllability,
  and spatial consistency. Our code and data will be released.
