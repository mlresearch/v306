title: 'OGPO: Sample Efficient Full-Finetuning of Generative Control Policies'
openreview: l1WWnr0ObL
abstract: Generative control policies (GCPs), such as diffusion- and flow-based control
  policies, have proved effective parameterizations for robot learning. This work
  introduces Off-policy Generative Policy Optimization (<b>OGPO</b>), a sample-efficient
  algorithm for finetuning GCPs that maintains off-policy critics to maximize data
  reuse and propagate policy gradients through the full generative process of the
  policy via a modified PPO objective, using critics as the terminal reward. <b>OGPO</b>
  achieves state-of-the-art performance on manipulation tasks spanning multi-task
  settings, high-precision insertion, and dexterous control. To our knowledge, it
  is also the only method that can <em>fine-tune poorly-initialized behavior cloning
  policies to near full task-success with no expert data in the online replay buffer</em>,
  and does so with <em>few task-specific hyperparameter tuning</em>. Through extensive
  investigations, we demonstrate that <b>OGPO</b> drastically outperforms alternative
  methods on policy steering and learning residual corrections, and identify the key
  mechanisms behind its performance. We further introduce practical stabilization
  tricks, including success-buffer regularization and two-sided conservative advantages
  to mitigate critic over-exploitation across state- and pixel-based settings. Beyond
  proposing <b>OGPO</b>, we conduct a systematic empirical study of GCP finetuning,
  identifying the stabilizing mechanisms and failure modes that govern successful
  off-policy full-policy improvement.
