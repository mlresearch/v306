title: 'WorldCache: Accelerating World Models for Free via Heterogeneous Token Caching'

abstract: 'Diffusion-based world models have shown strong potential for unified world
  simulation, but the iterative denoising remains too costly for interactive use and
  long-horizon rollouts. While feature caching can accelerate inference without training,
  we find that policies designed for single-modal diffusion transfer poorly to world
  models due to two world-model-specific obstacles: <em>token heterogeneity</em> from
  multi-modal coupling and spatial variation, and <em>non-uniform temporal dynamics</em>
  where a small set of hard tokens drives error growth, making uniform skipping either
  unstable or overly conservative. We propose <b>WorldCache</b>, a caching framework
  tailored to diffusion world models. We introduce <em>Curvature-guided Heterogeneous
  Token Prediction</em>, which uses a physics-grounded curvature score to estimate
  token predictability and applies a Hermite-guided damped predictor for chaotic tokens
  with abrupt direction changes. We also design <em>Chaotic-prioritized Adaptive Skipping</em>,
  which accumulates a curvature-normalized, dimensionless drift signal and recomputes
  only when bottleneck tokens begin to drift. Experiments on diffusion world models
  show that WorldCache delivers up to <b>3.7$\times$</b> end-to-end speedups while
  maintaining <b>98%</b> rollout quality, demonstrating the vast advantages and practicality
  of WorldCache in resource-constrained scenarios.'
