title: Efficient Stochastic Optimisation via Sequential Monte Carlo

abstract: The problem of optimising functions with intractable gradients frequently
  arises in machine learning and statistics, ranging from maximum marginal likelihood
  estimation procedures to fine-tuning of generative models. Stochastic approximation
  methods for this class of problems typically require inner sampling loops to obtain
  (biased) stochastic gradient estimates, which rapidly becomes computationally expensive.
  In this work, we develop sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) samplers for optimisation
  of functions with intractable gradients. Our approach replaces expensive inner sampling
  methods with efficient SMC approximations, which can result in significant computational
  gains. We establish convergence results for the basic recursions defined by our
  methodology which SMC samplers approximate. We demonstrate the effectiveness of
  our approach on the reward-tuning of energy-based models within various settings.
