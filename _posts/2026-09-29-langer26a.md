title: 'OpenTSLM: Time-Series Language Models for Reasoning over Multivariate Medical
  Text- and Time-Series Data'
openreview: FHDRzhKm0f
abstract: 'Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown strong capabilities in interpreting
  multimodal data but remain limited in handling time-series data. Addressing this
  gap could help translating longitudinal and wearable data into actionable insights
  and patient-facing digital health applications. We propose OpenTSLM, an open-source
  family of Time Series Language Models (TSLMs) integrating time-series as a native
  modality into pretrained LLMs, enabling natural-language prompting and reasoning
  over multiple time-series via either soft-prompting (OpenTSLM-SoftPrompt) or cross-attention
  (OpenTSLM-Flamingo). We introduce four datasets: M4-captioning, HAR-CoT (human activity
  recognition), Sleep-CoT (sleep staging), and ECG-QA-CoT (electrocardiogram question
  answering). Across tasks, OpenTSLM models outperform baselines like GPT-4o, reaching
  F1 scores of 69.88% in sleep staging and 67.64% in HAR; OpenTSLM-Flamingo also scales
  more efficiently in memory with longer or multiple time series. Expert evaluations
  with cardiologists show that OpenTSLM models exhibit strong reasoning capabilities
  on raw ECG data.'
