abstract: 'While the emerging field of agentic software engineering has spurred extensive
  research into post-training, this paradigm alone does not fully address the distribution
  mismatch between traditional static pre-training and dynamic deployment environments.
  In this paper, we instead investigate agentic mid-training as a scalable complementary
  approach. Central to our approach is <em>agent-native data</em> comprising two complementary
  components: <em>contextually-native trajectories</em> that preserve the complete
  information flow an agent experiences, offering broad coverage and diversity; and
  <em>environmentally-native trajectories</em> whose observations stem from actual
  tool invocations and test executions, providing interaction authenticity. On SWE-Bench
  Verified, our recipe outperforms the previous open software engineering mid-training
  recipe Kimi-Dev under two post-training settings with the same base model and agentic
  scaffold, while using fewer than half mid-training tokens (73.1B). Furthermore,
  our 32B and 72B models achieve state-of-the-art resolution rates of <b>56.1%</b>
  and <b>58.5%</b> among open agentic recipes using agentic scaffolds, despite starting
  from non-coder Qwen2.5 base models. We also observe performance gains on general
  code generation and scientific benchmarks. We open-source a significant portion
  of our datasets, recipes, and model checkpoints to facilitate further research.'
