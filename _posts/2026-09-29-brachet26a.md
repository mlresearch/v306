abstract: 'While deep learning is facing an homogenization across modalities led by
  Transformers, they are still challenged by shallow linear models in the time series
  forecasting task. Our hypothesis is that models should learn a direct link from
  past to future data points, which we identify as a learning dynamics capability.
  We develop an original $\texttt{PRO-DYN}$ nomenclature to analyze existing models
  through the lens of dynamics. Two observations thus emerge: <b>1.</b> under-performing
  architectures learn dynamics at most partially, <b>2.</b> the location of the dynamics
  block at the model end is of prime importance. Our systemic and empirical studies
  both confirm our observations on a set of performance-varying models with diverse
  backbones. We propose a simple plug-and-play methodology guiding model designs and
  improvements.'
