title: Semantic-Enriched Latent Visual Reasoning
openreview: DTuBIEhSF3
abstract: Multimodal latent-space reasoning replaces explicit “thinking with images”
  with reasoning in a compact latent space. Current methods mainly reshape low-level
  image features under visual supervision. Their latents capture appearance but carry
  limited high-level abstract semantics. We introduce Semantic-Enriched Latent Visual
  Reasoning (SLVR), a two-stage framework with a semantic attribute extraction head.
  In the first stage, this head converts region evidence into attribute-level semantic
  latents. These latents complement low-level visual features and support richer reasoning.
  In the second stage, Multi-query Group Relative Policy Optimization (M-GRPO) jointly
  optimizes multiple queries grounded in the same region. It builds on the invariance
  of intrinsic region attributes across different questions. M-GRPO constrains the
  degree of attribute variation across queries while preserving answer correctness.
  We construct SLV-Set, with approximately 400K region-level attribute annotations
  and 800K multi-query question answering samples, and introduce SV-QA for evaluation
  under semantic variation. Experiments show that SLVR improves the robustness and
  semantic consistency of latent visual reasoning. Our code and datasets are available
  at https://github.com/tinnel123666888/slvr.
