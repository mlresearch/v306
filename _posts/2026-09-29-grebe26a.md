abstract: 'While the rapid adoption of multimodal generative models offers immense
  potential, it has also increased the risks of harmful content synthesis, deepfakes,
  and copyright infringements. To address these challenges, concept erasure has emerged
  as a prospective safeguard. However, as the field gradually transitions from U-Net-based
  diffusion models to Rectified Flow Transformers, erasure research has struggled
  to keep pace. In this work, we introduce GEM, a simple but highly effective erasure
  framework for Rectified Flow models. As part of our contribution, we establish a
  principled bridge between trajectory-based unlearning grounded in Generative Flow
  Networks and classic teacher-guided erasure: we translate trajectory-based signals
  into a teacher-guided flow-matching setup that unifies the strengths of both paradigms.
  Concretely, a teacher provides complementary attraction and repulsion signals that
  we combine into a single geometric guidance objective, yielding targeted suppression
  of unwanted concepts while preserving benign generation.'
