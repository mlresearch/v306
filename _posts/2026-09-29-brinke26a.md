title: 'STFlow: Data-Coupled Flow Matching for Geometric Trajectory Simulation'
openreview: Wiwfiew7Y4
abstract: Simulating trajectories of dynamical systems is a fundamental problem in
  a wide range of fields such as molecular dynamics, biochemistry, and pedestrian
  dynamics. Machine learning has become an invaluable tool for scaling physics-based
  simulators and developing models directly from experimental data. In particular,
  recent advances in deep generative modeling and geometric deep learning enable probabilistic
  simulation by learning complex trajectory distributions while respecting intrinsic
  permutation and time-shift symmetries. However, trajectories of N-body systems are
  commonly characterized by high sensitivity to perturbations leading to bifurcations,
  as well as multi-scale temporal and spatial correlations. To address these challenges,
  we introduce STFlow (Spatio-Temporal Flow), a generative model based on graph neural
  networks and hierarchical convolutions. By incorporating data-dependent couplings
  within the Flow Matching framework, STFlow denoises starting from conditioned random-walks
  instead of Gaussian noise. This novel informed prior simplifies the learning task
  by reducing transport cost, increasing training and inference efficiency. We validate
  our approach on N-body systems, molecular dynamics, and human trajectory forecasting.
  Across these benchmarks, STFlow achieves the lowest prediction errors with fewer
  simulation steps and improved scalability.
