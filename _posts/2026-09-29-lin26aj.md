title: 'GR-LoRA: Gradient-Recycling Low-Rank Adaptation for Class-Incremental Learning'
openreview: MhMoUuoA1g
abstract: Pre-trained models with parameter-efficient fine-tuning have shown strong
  effectiveness in Class-Incremental Learning (CIL), which seeks to balance model
  plasticity and stability. In this context, orthogonality constraints can significantly
  enhance model stability, yet their reliance on subspace inevitably compromises model
  plasticity over long tasks. To address this, we propose Gradient-Recycling Low-Rank
  Adaptation (GR-LoRA), which reconciles stability and plasticity by recycling the
  gradients discarded in orthogonal projection. Specifically, GR-LoRA recycles post-decomposition
  non-orthogonal gradient components into task-specific lightweight modules and selects
  optimal module via entropy to improve plasticity, while incorporating local and
  global mismatch suppression to preserve stability by synthesizing out-of-distribution
  representations across all tasks. Theoretical analysis confirms that this recycling
  strategy preserves stability and improves plasticity. Experimental results from
  multiple CIL benchmarks verify the effectiveness and general applicability of GR-LoRA.
