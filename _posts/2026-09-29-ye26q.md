title: Depth-Progressive Monotonic Learning without Global Backpropagation
openreview: q3nEaVeIYZ
abstract: 'Backpropagation (BP) remains the dominant training paradigm for deep neural
  networks, yet its reliance on global gradient propagation fundamentally induces
  update locking problem, enforcing strong inter-layer dependencies in parameter updates.
  To address this limitation, we propose Depth-progressive Monotonic Learning (DMoL),
  a training scheme that assigns layer-wise local belief objectives and incrementally
  refines them across network depth, enabling unlocked parameter updates. As a result,
  DMoL supports dynamic modification of network depth during training, adapting to
  available compute and device resources while maintaining stable optimization. We
  provide theoretical guarantees that layer-wise local belief objectives improve monotonically
  with increasing depth and converge exponentially. Empirically, DMoL consistently
  matches or outperforms BP across diverse tasks, yielding a 4.3% accuracy gain on
  CIFAR-100, mitigating over-smoothing in deep graph neural networks (+37.5% on Cora),
  and reducing the final loss by over 35% in diffusion model training, highlighting
  its robustness and flexibility as an alternative to BP. The code is publicly available
  at: https://github.com/ychAlbert/DMoL.'
