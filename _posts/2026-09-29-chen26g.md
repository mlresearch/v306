title: 'FlashBlock: Attention Caching for Efficient Long-Context Block Diffusion'
openreview: 4jfuNNghPS
abstract: 'Generating long-form content, such as minute-long videos and extended texts,
  is increasingly important for modern generative models. Block diffusion improves
  inference efficiency via KV caching and block-wise causal inference and has been
  widely adopted in diffusion language models and video generation. However, in long-context
  settings, block diffusion still incurs substantial overhead from repeatedly computing
  attention over an ever-growing KV cache. We identify an underexplored property of
  block diffusion: cross-step redundancy of attention within a block. Our analysis
  shows that attention outputs from tokens outside the current block remain largely
  stable across diffusion steps, while block-internal attention varies significantly.
  Based on this observation, we propose FlashBlock, a cached block-external attention
  mechanism that reuses stable attention output, substantially reducing attention
  computation and KV cache access without modifying the diffusion process. Moreover,
  FlashBlock is orthogonal to sparse attention and can be combined as a complementary
  residual reuse strategy. When integrated, it substantially improves model accuracy
  under aggressive sparsification by offsetting much of the performance loss induced
  by sparsity. Experiments on diffusion language models and video generation demonstrate
  up to 1.44$\times$ higher token throughput and up to 1.6$\times$ reduction in attention
  time, with negligible impact on generation quality.'
