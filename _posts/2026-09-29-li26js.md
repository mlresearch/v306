abstract: 'The de facto approach in video object-centric learning maintains temporal
  consistency through learned dynamics modules that predict future object representations,
  called slots. We demonstrate that these predictors function as expensive approximations
  of discrete correspondence problems. Modern self-supervised vision backbones already
  encode instance-discriminative features that distinguish objects reliably. Exploiting
  these features eliminates the need for learned temporal prediction. We introduce
  Grounded Correspondence, a framework that replaces learned transition functions
  with deterministic bipartite matching. Slots initialize from salient regions in
  frozen backbone features. Frame-to-frame identity is maintained through Hungarian
  matching on slot representations. The approach requires zero learnable parameters
  for temporal modeling yet achieves competitive performance on MOVi-D, MOVi-E, and
  YouTube-VIS. Project page: https://magenta-sherbet-85b101.netlify.app/'
