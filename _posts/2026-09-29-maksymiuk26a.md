abstract: 'Macrocycles are ring-shaped molecules that offer a promising alternative
  to small-molecule drugs due to their enhanced selectivity and binding affinity against
  difficult targets. Despite their chemical value, they remain underexplored in generative
  modeling, likely owing to their scarcity in public datasets and the challenges of
  enforcing topological constraints in standard deep generative models. We introduce
  MacroGuide: Topological Guidance for Macrocycle Generation, a diffusion guidance
  mechanism that uses Persistent Homology to steer the sampling of pretrained molecular
  generative models toward the generation of macrocycles, in both unconditional and
  conditional (protein pocket) settings. At each denoising step, MacroGuide constructs
  a Vietoris-Rips complex from atomic positions and promotes ring formation by optimizing
  persistent homology features. Empirically, applying MacroGuide to pretrained diffusion
  models increases macrocycle generation rates from 1% to 99%, while matching or exceeding
  state-of-the-art performance on key quality metrics such as chemical validity, diversity,
  and PoseBusters checks. The implementation can be found at https://github.com/ala1705/MacroGuide.'
