title: Covariance estimation using Markov chain Monte Carlo

abstract: We investigate the complexity of covariance matrix estimation for Gibbs
  distributions based on dependent samples from a Markov chain. We show that when
  $$\pi$$ satisfies a Poincaré inequality and the chain possesses a spectral gap,
  we can achieve similar sample complexity using MCMC as compared to an estimator
  constructed using i.i.d. samples, with potentially much better query complexity.
  As an application of our methods, we show improvements for the query complexity
  in both constrained and unconstrained settings for concrete instances of MCMC. In
  particular, we provide guarantees regarding isotropic rounding procedures for sampling
  uniformly on convex bodies.
