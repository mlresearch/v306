abstract: Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) significantly enhances
  large language models (LLMs) reasoning but severely suffers from calibration degeneration,
  where models become excessively over-confident in incorrect answers. Previous studies
  devote to directly incorporating calibration objective into existing optimization
  target. However, our theoretical analysis demonstrates that there exists a fundamental
  gradient conflict between the optimization for maximizing policy accuracy and minimizing
  calibration error. Building on this insight, we propose DCPO, a simple yet effective
  framework that systematically decouples reasoning and calibration objectives. Extensive
  experiments demonstrate that our DCPO not only preserves accuracy on par with GRPO
  but also achieves the best calibration performance and substantially mitigates the
  over-confidence issue. Our study provides valuable insights and practical solution
  for more reliable LLM deployment.
