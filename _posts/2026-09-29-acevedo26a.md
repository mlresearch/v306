title: Differential syntactic and semantic encoding in LLMs
openreview: 2gSIm6WcKU
abstract: We study how syntactic and semantic information is encoded in inner layer
  representations of Large Language Models (LLMs), focusing on the very large DeepSeek-V3.
  We find that, by averaging hidden-representation vectors of sentences sharing syntactic
  structure or meaning, we obtain vectors that capture a significant proportion of
  the syntactic and semantic information contained in the representations. In particular,
  subtracting these syntactic and semantic “centroids” from sentence vectors strongly
  affects their similarity with syntactically and semantically matched sentences,
  respectively, suggesting that syntax and semantics are, at least partially, linearly
  encoded. We also find that the cross-layer encoding profiles of syntax and semantics
  are different, and that the two signals can to some extent be decoupled, suggesting
  differential encoding of these two types of linguistic information in LLM representations.
