title: Learning to Theorize the World from Observation
openreview: wsA8LgHU5U
abstract: 'What does it mean to understand the world? Contemporary world models often
  operationalize understanding as accurate future prediction in latent or observation
  space. Developmental cognitive science, however, suggests a different view: human
  understanding emerges through the construction of internal theories of how the world
  works, even before mature language is acquired. Inspired by this theory-building
  view of cognition, we introduce <em>Learning-to-Theorize</em>, a learning paradigm
  for inferring explicit explanatory theories of the world from raw, non-textual observations.
  We instantiate this paradigm with the <em>Neural Theorizer (NEO)</em>, a World Theory
  Model, that induces latent programs as a learned Language of Thought and executes
  them through a shared transition model. In NEO, a theory is represented as an executable,
  compositional program whose learned primitives can be systematically recombined
  to explain novel phenomena. Experiments show that this formulation enables explanation-driven
  generalization, allowing observations to be understood in terms of the programs
  that generate them. Our code is available at github.com/ahn-ml/learning-to-theorize'
