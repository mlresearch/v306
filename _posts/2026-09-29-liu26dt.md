abstract: 'Affective and cognitive disorders manifest as distributed, time-varying
  brain network dynamics across regions, channels, and time, challenging robust representation
  learning from EEG/sEEG for clinical diagnosis. We propose <b>RECTOR</b> (Masked
  <b>Re</b>gion–<b>C</b>hannel–<b>T</b>emp<b>or</b>al Modeling), an end-to-end self-supervised
  framework that unifies joint region-channel-temporal representation learning beyond
  fixed anatomical priors. At its core, <b>RECTOR-SA</b> is a hierarchical, block-sparse
  self-attention induced by Adaptive Functional Partitioning that evolves region structures
  from static anatomical definitions to adaptive functional regions. The self-supervision
  is driven by <b>Masked Topology and Representation Learning</b>, which jointly optimizes
  three complementary objectives: Masked Predictive Modeling, Topological Structure
  Modeling, and Cross-View Consistency. Across diverse benchmarks, RECTOR sets a new
  state-of-the-art in EEG emotion recognition and sEEG task-engagement classification.
  Crucially, its strong robustness to missing channels and cross-montage generalization
  underscores its potential for large-scale pre-training on heterogeneous EEG/sEEG,
  providing interpretable insights at both region and channel levels.'
