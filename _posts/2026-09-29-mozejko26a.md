title: 'PepCompass: Navigating Peptide Embedding Spaces Using Riemannian Geometry'
openreview: HENUU9VR8F
abstract: 'Antimicrobial peptide discovery is challenged by the astronomical size
  of peptide space and the relative scarcity of active peptides. While generative
  models provide latent maps of this space, they typically ignore decoder-induced
  geometry and rely on flat Euclidean metrics, making exploration distorted and inefficient.
  Existing manifold-based approaches assume fixed intrinsic dimensionality, which
  fails for real peptide data. We introduce <b>PepCompass</b>, a geometry-aware framework
  based on a <b>Union of $\kappa$-Stable Riemannian Manifolds</b> that captures local
  decoder geometry while maintaining computational stability. PepCompass performs
  global interpolation via <b>Potential-minimizing Geodesic Search (PoGS)</b> to bias
  discovery toward promising seeds and enables local exploration through <b>Second-Order
  Riemannian Brownian Efficient Sampling</b> and <b>Mutation Enumeration in Tangent
  Space</b>, which together form <b>Local Enumeration Bayesian Optimization (LE-BO)</b>.
  PepCompass achieves a 100% <em>in-vitro</em> validation rate: PoGS identifies four
  novel seeds and LE-BO optimizes them into 25 highly active, broad-spectrum peptides,
  demonstrating that geometry-informed exploration is a powerful paradigm for antimicrobial
  peptide design.'
