abstract: 'Operations Research practitioners debug infeasible models through an iterative
  process: inspecting Irreducible Infeasible Subsystems (<b>IIS</b>), identifying
  constraint conflicts, and repairing formulations until feasibility is restored.
  Existing LLM benchmarks mostly treat OR as one-shot translation from problem descriptions
  to solver code, omitting this diagnostic loop. We formalize infeasible-model repair
  as a solver-in-the-loop Markov Decision Process in which each action triggers solver
  re-execution and <b>IIS</b> recomputation, yielding deterministic, verifiable feedback.
  We introduce <b>ORLoopBench</b>, a benchmark suite with two components: <b>ORDebug</b>
  releases 5,362 LP/MILP repair instances, while <b>ORBias</b> evaluates closed-form
  operational decision rationality across inventory settings. Solver-verified RLVR
  training enables an 8B model to surpass frontier APIs on LP repair (95.3% vs 92.4%
  RR@5), improves diagnostic behavior, and transfers to MILP repair. The same evaluation
  exposes semantic drift in whole-model code regeneration: feasible regenerated MILPs
  can solve the wrong problem. Process-level evaluation with solver oracles enables
  targeted training for reliable OR self-correction.'
