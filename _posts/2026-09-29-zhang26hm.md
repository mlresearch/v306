title: 'Q-DiT4SR: Exploration of Detail-Preserving Diffusion Transformer Quantization
  for Real-World Image Super-Resolution'
openreview: e74RjRkirs
abstract: 'Recently, Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) have emerged in Real-World Image
  Super-Resolution (Real-ISR) to generate high-quality textures, yet their heavy inference
  burden hinders real-world deployment. While Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) is
  a promising solution for acceleration, existing methods in super-resolution mostly
  focus on U-Net architectures, whereas generic DiT quantization is typically designed
  for text-to-image tasks. Directly applying these methods to DiT-based super-resolution
  models leads to severe degradation of local textures. Therefore, we propose <b>Q-DiT4SR</b>,
  the first PTQ framework specifically tailored for DiT-based Real-ISR. We propose
  <b>H-SVD</b>, a hierarchical SVD that integrates a global low-rank branch with a
  local block-wise rank-1 branch under a matched parameter budget. We further propose
  <b>V</b>ariance-<b>a</b>ware <b>S</b>patio-<b>T</b>emporal <b>M</b>ixed <b>P</b>recision:
  <b>VaSMP</b> allocates cross-layer weight bit-widths in a data-free manner based
  on rate-distortion theory, while <b>VaTMP</b> schedules intra-layer activation precision
  across diffusion timesteps via dynamic programming (DP) with minimal calibration.
  Experiments on multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that our Q-DiT4SR achieves
  SOTA performance under both <b>W4A6</b> and <b>W4A4</b> settings. Notably, the W4A4
  quantization configuration reduces model size by <b>5.8</b>$\times$ and computational
  operations by <b>6.14</b>$\times$. Our code and models will be available at https://github.com/xunzhang1128/Q-DiT4SR.'
