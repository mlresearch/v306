title: Restoring Initial Noise Sensitivity in Text-to-Image Distillation via Geometric

abstract: 'Generative distillation significantly accelerates text-to-image (T2I) generation
  by compressing multi-step trajectories into few-step student models while preserving
  perceptual quality. However, existing methods primarily optimize efficiency and
  output fidelity, often neglecting critical properties of the original trajectory.
  In this work, we identify a key missing property: sensitivity to initial noise,
  whose degradation impairs downstream control methods relying on noise-based optimization
  and manipulation. We trace this issue to standard distillation objectives that enforce
  pointwise output alignment, inadvertently flattening the input-output landscape
  and suppressing the teacher’s local geometric structure. To address this, we propose
  Geometry-Aware Distillation (GAD), a sensitivity-preserving framework that aligns
  the local functional behavior of teacher and student models. Specifically, GAD matches
  Jacobian-vector products with respect to input noise, enabling the student to reproduce
  the teacher’s differential response to perturbations. Extensive experiments across
  multiple T2I paradigms and noise-driven control tasks demonstrate that GAD significantly
  restores sensitivity and improves diversity while maintaining high visual fidelity.
  Code is available at https://github.com/Hannah1102/GAD.'
