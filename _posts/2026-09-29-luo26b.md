abstract: Optimizing Conditional Value-at-risk (CVaR) using policy gradient (a.k.a
  CVaR-PG) faces significant challenges of sample inefficiency. This inefficiency
  stems from the fact that it focuses on tail-end performance and overlooks many sampled
  trajectories. We address this problem by augmenting CVaR with an expected quantile
  term. Quantile optimization admits a dynamic programming formulation that leverages
  all sampled data, thus improves sample efficiency. This does not alter the CVaR
  objective since CVaR corresponds to the expectation of quantile over the tail. Empirical
  results in domains with verifiable risk-averse behavior show that our algorithm
  within the Markovian policy class substantially improves upon CVaR-PG and consistently
  outperforms other existing methods.
