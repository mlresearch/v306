title: 'VidLaDA: Bidirectional Diffusion Large Language Models for Efficient Video
  Understanding'
openreview: efx3dOgAYw
abstract: 'Current Video Large Language Models (Video LLMs) typically encode frames
  via a vision encoder and employ an autoregressive (AR) LLM for understanding and
  generation. However, this AR paradigm inevitably faces a dual efficiency bottleneck:
  strictly unidirectional attention compromises <em>understanding efficiency</em>
  by hindering global spatiotemporal aggregation, while serial decoding restricts
  <em>generation efficiency</em>. To address this, we propose <b>VidLaDA</b>, a Video
  LLM based on Diffusion Language Models (DLMs) that leverages bidirectional attention
  to unlock comprehensive spatiotemporal modeling and decode tokens in parallel. To
  further mitigate the computational overhead of diffusion decoding, we introduce
  <b>MARS-Cache</b>, an acceleration strategy that prunes redundancy by combining
  asynchronous visual cache refreshing with frame-wise chunk attention. Experiments
  show VidLaDA rivals state-of-the-art AR baselines (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL and LLaVA-Video)
  and outperforms DLM baselines, with MARS-Cache delivering over 12x speedup without
  compromising accuracy. Code and checkpoints are open-sourced at https://github.com/ziHoHe/VidLaDA.'
