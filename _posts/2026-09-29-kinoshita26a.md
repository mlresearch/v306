title: Dataset Distillation Efficiently Encodes Low-Dimensional Representations from
  Gradient-Based Learning of Non-Linear Tasks
openreview: mCsBf1sm4p
abstract: Dataset distillation, a training-aware data compression technique, has recently
  attracted increasing attention as an effective tool for mitigating costs of optimization
  and data storage. However, progress remains largely empirical. Mechanisms underlying
  the extraction of task-relevant information from the training process and the efficient
  encoding of such information into synthetic data points remain elusive. In this
  paper, we theoretically analyze practical algorithms of dataset distillation applied
  to the gradient-based training of two-layer neural networks with width $L$. By focusing
  on a non-linear task structure called multi-index model, we prove that the low-dimensional
  structure of the problem is efficiently encoded into the resulting distilled data.
  This dataset reproduces a model with high generalization ability for a required
  memory complexity of $\tilde \Theta(r^2d+L)$, where $d$ and $r$ are the input and
  intrinsic dimensions of the task. To the best of our knowledge, this is one of the
  first theoretical works that include a specific task structure, leverage its intrinsic
  dimensionality to quantify the compression rate and study dataset distillation implemented
  solely via gradient-based algorithms.
