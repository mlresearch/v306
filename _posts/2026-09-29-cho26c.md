abstract: Inference on the conditional mean function (CMF) is central to tasks from
  adaptive experimentation to optimal treatment assignment and algorithmic fairness
  auditing. In this work, we provide a novel asymptotic anytime-valid test for a CMF
  global null (e.g., that all conditional means are zero) and contrasts between CMFs,
  enabling experimenters to make high confidence decisions at <em>any</em> time during
  the experiment beyond a minimum sample size. We provide mild conditions under which
  our tests achieve (i) asymptotic type-I error guarantees, (i) power one, and, unlike
  past tests, (iii) optimal sample complexity relative to a Gaussian location testing.
  By inverting our tests, we show how to construct function-valued asymptotic confidence
  sequences for the CMF and contrasts thereof. Experiments on both synthetic and real-world
  data show our method is well-powered across various distributions while preserving
  the nominal error rate under continuous monitoring.
