title: Unified Time Series Explanations via Amortized Optimization and Instance-level
  Multi-Expert Knowledge Distillation
openreview: dspqiZrVPK
abstract: Deep neural networks (DNNs) achieve high accuracy on time series classification
  (TSC) but remain opaque, hindering deployment in sensitive domains. Existing post-hoc
  TSC explanation methods rely on a single attribution perspective and incur high
  per-instance computational cost, limiting real-time use. We propose XMA, a framework
  that unifies multiple post-hoc explainers at the instance level and amortizes the
  cost of explanation into a learned inference pass. <b>Instance-level Multi-Expert
  Knowledge Distillation (IMEKD)</b> selects, per instance, the attribution map with
  the highest combined faithfulness and robustness score from a pool of XAI experts.
  <b>Objective-Regularized Amortized Optimization Explanation (ORAOE)</b> trains an
  explainer DNN to reproduce this instance-best supervision while directly optimizing
  differentiable faithfulness and robustness objectives, letting the student refine
  beyond any individual teacher. <b>Faithfulness-Preserving Segmentation (FPS)</b>
  converts point-wise maps into contiguous segments without changing their faithfulness
  score. Across four synthetic datasets, MIT-ECG with cardiologist-verified ground
  truth, and 11 multivariate UEA benchmarks on three DNN architectures, XMA attains
  the best AUPRC on all synthetic and MIT-ECG benchmarks among compared methods, and
  the highest faithfulness on 7 of 11 and robustness on 8 of 11 UEA datasets, while
  amortizing explanation into a lightweight learned explainer rather than relying
  on per-instance optimization.
