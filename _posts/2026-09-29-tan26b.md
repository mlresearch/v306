title: 'VideoVeritas: AI-Generated Video Detection via Perception Pretext Reinforcement

abstract: The growing capability of video generation poses escalating security risks,
  making reliable detection increasingly essential. In this paper, we introduce <b>VideoVeritas</b>,
  a framework that integrates fine-grained perception and fact-based reasoning. We
  observe that while current multi-modal large language models (MLLMs) exhibit strong
  reasoning capacity, their granular perception ability remains limited. To mitigate
  this, we introduce <em>Joint Preference Alignment</em> and <em>Perception Pretext
  Reinforcement Learning (PPRL)</em>. Specifically, rather than directly optimizing
  for detection task, we adopt general spatiotemporal grounding and self-supervised
  object counting in the RL stage, enhancing detection performance with simple <em>perception
  pretext tasks</em>. To facilitate robust evaluation, we further introduce <b>MintVid</b>,
  a light yet high-quality dataset containing 3K videos from 9 state-of-the-art generators,
  along with a real-world collected subset that has factual errors in content. Experimental
  results demonstrate that existing methods tend to bias towards either <em>superficial</em>
  reasoning or <em>mechanical</em> analysis, while <b>VideoVeritas</b> achieves more
  balanced performance across diverse benchmarks.
