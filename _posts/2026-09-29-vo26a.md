title: 'Hölder++: Improving the Quality-Coherence Trade-off in Multimodal VAEs'
openreview: LQ3TocqrTh
abstract: 'Existing approaches for multimodal variational autoencoders (VAEs) face
  a trade-off between generative quality and coherence—i.e., they struggle to generate
  realistic and diverse samples that, at the same time, are semantically consistent
  across modalities. A recent work shows that using a simple approximation to Hölder
  pooling as an aggregation method improves coherence over the SOTA MMVAE+, despite
  assuming a single shared representation across all modalities. Yet, it slightly
  compromises sample diversity. Inspired by this insight, we propose Hölder++, a novel
  multimodal VAE that improves the generative quality-coherence trade-off through:
  (i) the first implementation of <em>Hölder pooling without any approximation</em>
  for multimodal VAEs; (ii) an extended architecture that models <em>distinct shared
  and private</em> (i.e., modality-specific) representations (Hölder+); and (iii)
  <em>hierarchical inference</em> that further enhances the disentanglement between
  the shared and private representations (Hölder++). Our experiments corroborate that
  Hölder++ consistently improves the generative quality-coherence trade-off, yields
  more structured latent spaces, and learns shared representations that are informative
  for downstream tasks.'
