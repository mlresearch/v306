title: 'Position: Mechanisms for Aggregated Individual Reporting Should be Established
  for Post-Deployment Evaluation'

abstract: The need for developing model evaluations beyond static benchmarking, especially
  in the post-deployment phase, is now well-understood. At the same time, concerns
  about the concentration of power in deployed AI systems have sparked a keen interest
  in "democratic" or "public" AI. In this work, we bring these two ideas together
  by proposing mechanisms for aggregated individual reporting (AIR), a framework for
  post-deployment evaluation that relies on individual reports from the public. An
  AIR mechanism allows those who interact with a specific, deployed (AI) system to
  report when they feel that they may have experienced something problematic; these
  reports are then aggregated over time, with the goal of evaluating the relevant
  system in a fine-grained manner. <b>This position paper argues that individual experiences
  should be understood as an integral part of post-deployment evaluation, and that
  the scope of our proposed aggregated individual reporting mechanism is a practical
  path to that end.</b> On the one hand, individual reporting can identify substantively
  novel insights about safety and performance; on the other, aggregation can be uniquely
  useful for informing action. From a normative perspective, the post-deployment phase
  completes a missing piece in the conversation about "democratic" AI. As a pathway
  to implementation, we provide a workflow of concrete design decisions and pointers
  to areas for future research.
