title: Can local learning match self-supervised backpropagation?
openreview: 0QsbcZKCQ0
abstract: 'While end-to-end self-supervised learning with backpropagation (global
  BP-SSL) has become central for training modern AI systems, theories of local self-supervised
  learning (local-SSL) have struggled to build functional representations in deep
  neural networks. To establish a link between global and local rules, we first develop
  a theory for deep linear networks: We identify conditions for local-SSL algorithms
  (like Forward-forward or CLAPP) to implement exactly the same weight update as a
  global BP-SSL. Starting from the theoretical insights, we then develop novel variants
  of local-SSL algorithms to approximate global BP-SSL in deep non-linear convolutional
  neural networks. Variants that improve the similarity between updates of local-SSL
  with those of global BP-SSL also show better performance on image datasets (CIFAR-10,
  STL-10, Tiny ImageNet, and ImageNet). The best local-SSL rule with the CLAPP loss
  function matches the performance of a comparable global BP-SSL with InfoNCE or CPC-like
  loss functions, and improves upon state-of-the-art for layer-wise SSL on these benchmarks.'
