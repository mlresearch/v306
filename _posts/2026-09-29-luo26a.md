title: 'Attention Sparsity is Input-Stable: Training-Free Sparse Attention for Video
  Generation via Offline Sparsity Profiling and Online QK Co-Clustering'

abstract: 'Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) achieve strong video generation quality but
  suffer from high inference cost due to dense 3D attention, leading to the development
  of sparse attention technologies to improve efficiency. However, existing training-free
  sparse attention methods in video generation still face two unresolved limitations:
  <em>ignoring layer heterogeneity in attention pruning</em> and <em>ignoring query-key
  coupling in block partitioning</em>, which hinder a better quality-speedup trade-off.
  In this work, we uncover a critical insight that <b>the attention sparsity of each
  layer is its intrinsic property, with minor effects across different inputs</b>.
  Motivated by this, we propose <b>SVOO</b>, a training-free <b>S</b>parse attention
  framework for fast <b>V</b>ideo generation via <b>O</b>ffline layer-wise sparsity
  profiling and <b>O</b>nline bidirectional co-clustering. Specifically, SVOO adopts
  a two-stage paradigm: (i) offline layer-wise sensitivity profiling to derive intrinsic
  per-layer pruning levels, and (ii) online block-wise sparse attention via a novel
  bidirectional co-clustering algorithm. Extensive experiments on seven widely used
  video generation models demonstrate that SVOO achieves a superior quality-speedup
  trade-off over state-of-the-art methods, delivering up to 1.93$\times$ speedup while
  maintaining a PSNR of up to 29 dB on Wan2.1.'
