title: Joint Enhancement and Classification using Coupled Diffusion Models of Signals

openreview: v9rdrvA4Yx
abstract: 'Robust classification in noisy environments remains a fundamental challenge
  in machine learning. Standard approaches typically treat signal enhancement and
  classification as separate, sequential stages: first enhancing the signal and then
  applying a classifier. This approach fails to leverage the semantic information
  in the classifier’s output during denoising. In this work, we propose a general,
  domain-agnostic framework that integrates two interacting diffusion models: one
  operating on the input signal and the other on the classifier’s output logits, without
  requiring any retraining or fine-tuning of the classifier. This coupled formulation
  enables mutual guidance, where the enhancing signal refines the class estimation
  and, conversely, the evolving class logits guide the signal reconstruction towards
  discriminative regions of the manifold. We introduce three strategies to effectively
  model the joint distribution of the input and the logit. We evaluated our joint
  enhancement method for image classification and automatic speech recognition. The
  proposed framework surpasses traditional sequential enhancement baselines, delivering
  robust and flexible improvements in classification accuracy under diverse noise
  conditions.'
