abstract: 'We introduce a new context-enriched, multimodal time series forecasting
  benchmark TimesX. TimesX contains a wide selection of high-quality real-world time
  series with diverse domains and textual contexts obtained from an automated data
  generation pipeline, which helps address three main issues of existing multimodal
  forecasting benchmarks: (1) poor generalization due to the small scale and synthetic
  nature of benchmark data, (2) very limited types of textual contexts in the benchmarks,
  and (3) an inability to mitigate data leakage in evaluation. We conduct a thorough
  empirical study of zero-shot multimodal forecasting approaches on TimesX. Our results
  suggest that many approaches that perform well on existing benchmarks may fail on
  TimesX. In contrast, simple ensemble methods that leverage rich textual context
  accompanying time-series can outperform strong baselines on the TimesX benchmark.'
