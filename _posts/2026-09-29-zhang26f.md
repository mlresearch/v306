title: Failure-Driven Workflow Refinement

abstract: 'Workflow optimization for tool-using LLM agents is often cast as global
  search over candidate graphs, scored by a scalar metric. This collapses rich, multi-step
  failure traces into binary outcomes, obscuring recurring failure structure and making
  refinement inefficient. We reframe optimization as <em>distributional refinement</em>:
  each workflow induces a density over a <b>Failure Signature Space</b> $\mathcal{F}$,
  and the goal is to minimize its <b>Expected Failure Mass</b>. We propose <b>CE-Graph</b>,
  which maintains a counterexample pool, estimates dense failure modes, and applies
  operator-constrained graph edits via a <b>Propose-and-Verify</b> loop with a convergence-aware
  stopping rule. Across math, code, and QA benchmarks, CE-Graph improves robustness
  while reducing optimization cost compared to strong workflow-search baselines, suggesting
  reliability emerges from learning and reshaping failure landscapes rather than merely
  maximizing aggregate success rates.'
