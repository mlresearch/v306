title: 'BioDynaSpec: Harmonic-Guided Spatio-Spectral Autoregressive Diffusion for
  Protein Dynamics Generation'
openreview: TwMRZPkn4e
abstract: 'Generating long-horizon molecular dynamics (MD) is difficult due to error
  accumulation in time-domain autoregressive models, which causes drift, and fixed
  step-size constraints on temporal resolution. We propose <b>BioDynaSpec</b>, which
  reformulates protein dynamics as spatio-spectral generation: <b>Independent Windowed
  Fourier Decomposition (IWFD)</b> decomposes trajectories into window-wise spectral
  representations, and a generator combines low-to-high frequency autoregression with
  diffusion denoising to reconstruct continuous motion. This formulation is motivated
  by <b>a local near-equilibrium view of protein dynamics</b>: after per-window alignment,
  fluctuations around an anchor conformation are better characterized in spectral
  space, where local mode structure is more explicit than in frame-wise coordinates.
  To improve cross-residue and cross-frequency consistency, we introduce <b>Inter-Residue
  Frequency Coupling (IRFC)</b>, a learnable Gaussian distance bias in attention that
  injects a resonance-inspired structural prior. On ATLAS, BioDynaSpec improves 250-frame
  trajectory generation with $R_{250}=1.509$ {Å}, where $R_s$ denotes the mean per-frame
  C$\alpha$-RMSE over the first $s$ frames after alignment, reducing error by 60.4%
  versus MDGEN and 57.2% versus ProAR, while achieving the best PCA-2D displacement-profile
  correlation and stepwise distribution matching. For equilibrium conformational sampling,
  it achieves Root Mean $W_2=1.31$, MD PCA $W_2=0.90$, and Joint PCA $W_2=1.19$, improving
  over the next best method by 50.03%, 35.25%, and 47.58%, respectively. It also improves
  near-equilibrium local-dynamics and covariance consistency, achieving PCA-PSD-LogCorr
  $=0.817$ and CFRE $=0.989$, corresponding to a 21.9% gain and a 36.7% reduction
  over the next best method, respectively. The source code is available at https://github.com/Linmj-Judy/BioDynaSpec.git.'
