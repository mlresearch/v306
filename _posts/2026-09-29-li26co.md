abstract: 'Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are increasingly deployed in stateless
  systems, such as autonomous driving and robotics. This paper investigates a novel
  threat: Semantic-Aware Hijacking. We explore the feasibility of hijacking multiple
  stateless decisions simultaneously using a single universal perturbation. We introduce
  the Semantic-Aware Universal Perturbation (SAUP), which acts as a semantic router,
  "actively" perceiving input semantics and routing them to distinct, attacker-defined
  targets. To achieve this, we conduct a theoretical and empirical analysis on the
  geometric properties in the latent space. Guided by these insights, we propose the
  Semantic-Oriented (SORT) optimization strategy and annotate a new dataset with fine-grained
  semantics to evaluate performance. Extensive experiments on three representative
  MLLMs demonstrate the fundamental feasibility of this attack, achieving a 66% attack
  success rate over five targets using a single frame against Qwen.'
