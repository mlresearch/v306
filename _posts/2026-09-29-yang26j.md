abstract: 'Protein-protein interactions (PPIs) are essential for many biological processes.
  However, existing PPI prediction approaches suffer from two major limitations: they
  overlook the hierarchical organization of proteins, particularly meso-scale motifs
  that critically regulate PPIs, and fail to effectively integrate sequence, structure,
  and function modalities. To address these limitations, we propose MMM-PPI, a Hierarchical
  Motif-based Multi-Modal protein Encoder for PPI Prediction that constructs PPI embeddings
  in a bottom-up multi-modal manner across three scales. At the micro-scale, we encode
  three modal residue features; at the meso-scale, a novel multimodal motif encoder
  aggregates residues into spatially-informed motif embeddings; at the macro-scale,
  a multimodal protein encoder integrates motifs into protein embeddings by jointly
  modeling motif importance and inter-modal correlations. The pre-trained encoder
  can be used off-the-shelf for large-scale PPI prediction. Extensive experiments
  on multiple PPI datasets show that MMM-PPI outperforms state-of-the-art multi-label
  PPI prediction models, particularly under challenging data partitions and limited
  data scenarios. Codes are in https://github.com/yzf-code/MMM-PPI.'
