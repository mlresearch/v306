abstract: 'We design efficient approximation algorithms for maximizing the expectation
  of the supremum of families of Gaussian random variables. In particular, let $OPT:=\max_{\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n}\mathbb{E}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\max_{i\in
  S_j} X_i$, where $X_i$ are Gaussian, $S_j\subset[n]$ and $\sum_i\sigma_i^2=1$, then
  our theoretical results include: - We characterize the optimal variance allocation
  – it concentrates on a small subset of variables as $|S_j|$ increases, - A polynomial
  time approximation scheme (PTAS) for computing OPT when $m=1$, and - An $O(\log
  n)$ approximation algorithm for computing OPT for general $m>1$.'
