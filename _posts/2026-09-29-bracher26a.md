abstract: We consider problems of parameter estimation where design variables can
  be actively optimized to maximize information gain. To this end, we introduce JADAI,
  a framework that jointly amortizes Bayesian adaptive design and inference by training
  a policy, a history network, and an inference network end-to-end. The networks minimize
  a generic loss that aggregates incremental reductions in posterior error along experimental
  sequences without density evaluations. Inference networks are instantiated with
  diffusion models that can approximate high-dimensional and multimodal posteriors
  at every experimental step. JADAI achieves superior or competitive performance across
  adaptive design benchmarks.
