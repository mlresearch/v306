title: Do Neural Operators Forget Geometry? The Forgetting Hypothesis in Deep Operator

abstract: 'Neural operators perform well on structured domains, yet their behaviour
  on irregular geometries remains poorly understood. We show that this limitation
  is not merely an encoding issue, but a depth-wise failure mode inherent to deep
  operator architectures. We formalise the <em>Geometric Forgetting Hypothesis</em>:
  due to the Markovian structure of operator layers and their reliance on global mixing
  mechanisms, neural operators progressively lose access to domain geometry as depth
  increases. Using layer-wise geometric probing, we demonstrate that both spectral
  and attention-based operators systematically lose geometric fidelity. We show that
  this geometric forgetting degrades accuracy, stability, and generalisation. To counteract
  it, we introduce a lightweight <em>geometry memory injection</em> mechanism that
  restores geometric constraints at intermediate depths with minimal architectural
  overhead. This simple intervention consistently mitigates forgetting and exposes
  a <em>geometric shortcut</em> instability in transformer-based operators, revealing
  that geometric retention is a structural requirement rather than a design choice.'
