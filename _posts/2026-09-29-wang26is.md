title: 'GradPower: Powering Gradients for Faster Language Model Pre-Training'

abstract: 'We propose <b>GradPower</b>, a lightweight gradient-transformation technique
  for accelerating language model pre-training. Given a gradient vector $\boldsymbol{g}=(g_
  {i})_ {i}$, GradPower first applies the elementwise sign-power transformation: $
  \varphi_ p(\boldsymbol{g}) = \left({\rm sign}(g_ i)|g_ i|^p\right)_ {i} $ for a
  fixed $p>0$, and then feeds the transformed gradient into a base optimizer. Notably,
  GradPower requires only a <b>single-line code change</b> and no modifications to
  the base optimizer’s internal logic, including the hyperparameters. When applied
  to AdamW (termed <b>AdamWPower</b>), GradPower consistently achieves lower terminal
  loss across diverse architectures (LLaMA, Qwen2MoE), parameter scales (66M to 2B),
  datasets (C4, OpenWebText), and learning-rate schedules (cosine, warmup-stable-decay).
  The most pronounced gains are observed when training modern mixture-of-experts models
  with warmup-stable-decay schedules. GradPower also integrates seamlessly with other
  state-of-the-art optimizers, such as Muon, yielding further improvements. Finally,
  we provide theoretical analyses that reveal the underlying mechanism of GradPower
  and highlight the influence of gradient noise.'
