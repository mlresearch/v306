title: Mitigating Noise-Induced Layout Priors for Object Counting in Diffusion Models
openreview: NJHPCVfC2n
abstract: 'Despite remarkable progress in text-to-image diffusion models, accurately
  generating the specified number of objects remains a persistent challenge. We identify
  the initial noise as a primary determinant of spatial layout formation, with early-stage
  cross-attention serving as the key mechanism that mediates the propagation of noise-induced
  structures throughout the denoising process. We characterize this phenomenon as
  <b><em>Noise-Induced Layout Prior</em></b>. Leveraging this insight, we propose
  a novel training-free framework for object counting in diffusion models. Our approach
  consists of two key components: (1) a <em>Count-Aware Noise Adjustment Strategy</em>,
  which explicitly manipulates the initial latent noise to align layout formation
  with the target object count, and (2) an <em>Attention-Guided Layout Consistency
  Strategy</em>, which performs test-time optimization on early-stage cross-attention
  to further stabilize layout formation during denoising. Extensive experiments on
  both single-category and multi-category benchmarks demonstrate that our method consistently
  outperforms strong diffusion baselines and state-of-the-art object count control
  methods in terms of counting accuracy and image quality. Code Release: https://github.com/lxlong1201/Mitigate_Noise_Prior.'
