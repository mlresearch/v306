title: Improving Classifier-Free Guidance of Flow Matching via Manifold Projection
openreview: IPp3LD6u16
abstract: Classifier-free guidance (CFG) is a widely used technique for controllable
  generation in diffusion and flow-based models. Despite its empirical success, CFG
  relies on a heuristic linear extrapolation that is often sensitive to the guidance
  scale. In this work, we provide a principled interpretation of CFG through the lens
  of optimization. We demonstrate that the velocity field in flow matching corresponds
  to the gradient of a sequence of smoothed distance functions, which guides latent
  variables toward the scaled target image set. This perspective reveals that the
  standard CFG formulation is an approximation of this gradient, where the prediction
  gap, the discrepancy between conditional and unconditional outputs, governs guidance
  sensitivity. Leveraging this insight, we reformulate the CFG sampling as a homotopy
  optimization with a manifold constraint. This formulation necessitates a manifold
  projection step, which we implement via an incremental gradient descent scheme during
  sampling. To improve computational efficiency and stability, we further enhance
  this iterative process with Anderson Acceleration without requiring additional model
  evaluations. Our proposed methods are training-free and consistently refine generation
  fidelity, prompt alignment, and robustness to the guidance scale. We validate their
  effectiveness across diverse benchmarks, demonstrating significant improvements
  on large-scale models such as DiT-XL-2-256, Flux, and Stable Diffusion 3.5. Code
  is available at https://github.com/LeonSuZhengYi/CFG-MP.
