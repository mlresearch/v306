title: 'PhaseCoder: Microphone Geometry-Agnostic Spatial Audio Understanding for Multimodal
  LLMs'
openreview: tU3raVqvYe
abstract: Current multimodal large language models (LLMs) process audio as a mono
  stream, ignoring the rich spatial information essential for embodied AI. Conversely,
  existing spatial audio models are constrained to fixed microphone geometries, preventing
  their deployment across diverse devices. We present <b>PhaseCoder</b>, a transformer-only
  spatial audio encoder that is inherently agnostic to microphone geometry. By taking
  raw multichannel audio and microphone coordinates as inputs, PhaseCoder performs
  accurate localization and produces robust spatial embeddings. We demonstrate that
  the Gemma 3n LLM can be fine-tuned to process and reason over the "Spatial Audio
  Tokens" produced by our encoder. PhaseCoder achieves state-of-the-art results on
  microphone-invariant localization benchmarks and, for the first time, enables an
  LLM to perform complex spatial reasoning and targeted transcription tasks from an
  arbitrary microphone array.
