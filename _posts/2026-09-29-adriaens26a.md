abstract: The Bad Triangle Transversal (BTT) problem asks for the smallest set of
  edges that need to be removed from a given signed graph, so that the resulting graph
  does not have a bad triangle. Here, a bad triangle is a triangle with exactly one
  negative edge. Several 2-approximations for BTT are proposed in this paper. On the
  hardness side, we show that BTT is NP-hard to approximate with factor better than
  $\frac{2137}{2136}$ on complete graphs. Our reduction also works for Correlation
  Clustering (CC), the Cluster Deletion problem (CD) and the Minimum Strong Triadic
  Closure problem (MinSTC). Lastly, we show that the BTT and CC optima are within
  a factor of 3/2 in complete graphs, by describing a pivot procedure that transforms
  transversals into clusters.
