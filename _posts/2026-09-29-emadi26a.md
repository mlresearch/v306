title: Rank-Aware Spectral Bounds on Attention Logits for Stable Low-Precision Training
openreview: JUM3VsJ4GO
abstract: 'Attention scores in transformers are bilinear forms $S_{ij} = x_i^\top
  M x_j / \sqrt{d_h}$ whose maximum magnitude governs overflow risk in low-precision
  training. We derive a <em>rank-aware concentration inequality</em>: when the interaction
  matrix $M = W^Q W^{K\top}$ has rank $r \ll d$, tail probabilities for $\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|$
  decay as $\exp(-d^{2}\alpha^{2}/(\gamma r))$ rather than $\exp(-d\alpha^{2})$, where
  $\gamma > 1$. For transformer attention where $r = d_h$, this yields $8$–$28\times$
  tighter concentration than rank-agnostic bounds in modern architectures. We apply
  this result to FP8 training, deriving <em>geometry-aware scale factors</em> that
  provide principled overflow guarantees without observing activations. The method
  computes per-layer scales from the spectral norm $\|W^Q W^{K\top}\|_2$ via implicit
  power iteration, includes a grouped query attention formulation that avoids key
  expansion, and remains compatible with fused attention kernels. Across GPT-2 XL
  to Llama-2-70B, geometry-aware scaling eliminates overflows in transient scenarios
  where delayed scaling fails, while achieving comparable downstream MMLU accuracy.'
