abstract: 'Generalization and approximation capabilities of message passing graph
  neural networks (MPNNs) are often studied by defining a compact metric on a space
  of input graphs under which MPNNs are equicontinuous. Such analyses are of two varieties:
  1) when the metric space includes graphs of unbounded sizes, the theory is only
  appropriate for dense graphs, and, 2) when studying sparse graphs, the metric space
  only includes graphs of uniformly bounded size. In this work, we present a unified
  approach, defining a compact metric on the space of graphs of all sizes, both sparse
  and dense, under which MPNNs are equicontinuous. This leads to more powerful universal
  approximation theorems and generalization bounds than previous works. The theory
  is based on, and extends, a recent approach to graph limit theory called graphop
  analysis.'
