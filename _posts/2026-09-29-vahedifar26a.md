abstract: Continual learning enables neural networks to learn tasks sequentially without
  forgetting previously acquired knowledge. However, neural networks suffer from catastrophic
  forgetting, where learning new tasks degrades performance on earlier ones. We address
  this problem with <b>Shapley Neuron Valuation (SNV)</b>, a principled framework
  that quantifies Neuron importance in continual learning, grounded in cooperative
  game theory. SNV selectively freezes important Neurons while keeping others plastic,
  enabling <b>buffer-free</b> continual learning without expanding architecture. Experiments
  on ImageNet-1k show that SNV consistently outperforms existing buffer-free methods.
  In particular, SNV improves accuracy by <b>+2.88%</b> in the class incremental learning
  and <b>+6.46%</b> in the task incremental learning scenarios compared to the second
  baseline.
