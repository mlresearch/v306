abstract: We investigate the role of network architecture in shaping the inductive
  biases of modern score-based generative models. To this end, we introduce the Score
  Anisotropy Directions (SADs), architecture-dependent directions that reveal how
  different networks preferentially capture data structure. Our analysis suggests
  that SADs form adaptive bases aligned with the architecture’s output geometry, providing
  a principled way to predict generalization ability in score models prior to training.
  Through both synthetic data and standard image benchmarks, we demonstrate that SADs
  reliably capture fine-grained model behavior and correlate with downstream performance,
  as measured by Wasserstein metrics. Our work offers a new lens for explaining and
  predicting directional biases of generative models.
