abstract: We propose <b>DualOptim+</b>, a novel optimization framework for improving
  machine unlearning in large language models. It introduces a base state to capture
  common representations shared by forgetting and retaining objectives and delta states
  to preserve objective-specific residuals. This architecture allows the optimizer
  to adaptively bridge shared and decoupled states based on the directional conflict
  between forgetting and retaining gradients. We further introduce DualOptim+ 8bit,
  a quantized variant that reduces memory overhead without compromising performance.
  Extensive experiments across fictitious and real-world unlearning, safety alignment,
  and multi-task learning tasks demonstrate that DualOptim+ consistently achieves
  a superior trade-off between different objectives. Codes are available at https://github.com/CityU-MLO/DualOptimPlus.
