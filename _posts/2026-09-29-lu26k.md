title: 'Geometric Reciprocity: Unlocking Self-Supervision for Stereoscopic Video Generation'

abstract: 'Monocular-to-stereo conversion synthesizes stereoscopic content from 2D
  videos for immersive 3D experiences. In modern Depth-Image-Based Rendering (DIBR)
  approaches, stereo inpainting of disocclusions is the critical bottleneck. Training-based
  methods achieve superior quality but rely on scarce stereo pairs or synthetic data
  with domain gaps. We address this through the first self-supervised framework learning
  from monocular videos via cycle consistency. Our key contribution is the <b>Geometric
  Reciprocity Theorem (GRT)</b>: under the nearest-neighbor DIBR formulation, the
  disocclusion mask when synthesizing a target view equals the mask of pixels lost
  when warping back from target to source, enabling analytical computation of test-time
  disocclusion masks directly from monocular images. This yields train-test consistency
  for the stated warping formulation, supporting self-supervised learning from unlimited
  monocular videos and substantial improvements over training-free and supervised
  state-of-the-art methods. Project page: https://visual-ai.github.io/grt/'
