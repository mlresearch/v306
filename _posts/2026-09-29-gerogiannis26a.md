abstract: We introduce a practical, black-box framework termed Detection Augmented
  Learning (DAL) for the problem of piecewise stationary bandits without knowledge
  of the underlying non-stationarity. DAL accepts any stationary bandit algorithm
  with order-optimal regret as input and augments it with a change detector, enabling
  applicability to all common bandit variants. Extensive experimentation demonstrates
  that DAL consistently surpasses all state-of-the-art methods across diverse non-stationary
  scenarios, including synthetic benchmarks and real-world datasets, underscoring
  its versatility and scalability. We provide theoretical insights into DAL’s strong
  empirical performance, complemented by thorough empirical validation.
