abstract: The mean-field Langevin dynamics (MFLD) minimizes an entropy-regularized
  nonlinear convex functional on the Wasserstein space over $\mathbb{R}^d$, and has
  gained attention recently as a model for the gradient descent dynamics of interacting
  particle systems such as infinite-width two-layer neural networks. However, many
  problems of interest have constrained domains, which are not solved by existing
  mean-field algorithms due to the global diffusion term. We study the optimization
  of probability measures constrained to a convex subset of $\mathbb{R}^d$ by proposing
  the <em>mirror mean-field Langevin dynamics</em> (MMFLD), an extension of MFLD to
  the mirror Langevin framework. We obtain linear convergence guarantees for the continuous
  MMFLD via a uniform log-Sobolev inequality, and uniform-in-time propagation of chaos
  results for its time- and particle-discretized counterpart.
