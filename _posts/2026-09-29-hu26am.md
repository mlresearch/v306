title: Human-in-the-Loop Policy Optimization for Preference-Based Multi-Objective
  Reinforcement Learning

abstract: Multi-objective reinforcement learning (MORL) seeks policies that effectively
  balance conflicting objectives. However, presenting many diverse policies without
  accounting for the decision maker’s (DM’s) preferences can overwhelm the decision-making
  process. On the other hand, accurately specifying preferences in advance is often
  unrealistic. To address these challenges, we introduce a human-in-the-loop MORL
  framework that interactively discovers preferred policies during optimization. Our
  approach proactively learns the DM’s implicit preferences in real time, requiring
  no a priori knowledge. Importantly, we integrate this preference learning directly
  into a parallel optimization framework, balancing exploration and exploitation to
  identify high-quality policies aligned with the DM’s preferences. Evaluations on
  a complex quadrupedal robot simulation environment demonstrate that, with only interactions,
  our proposed method can identify policies aligned with human preferences, e.g.,
  running like a dog. Further experiments on seven MuJoCo tasks and a multi-microgrid
  system design task against eight state-of-the-art MORL algorithms fully demonstrate
  the effectiveness of our proposed framework. Demonstrations and full experiments
  are in https://sites.google.com/view/pbmorl/home.
