title: Hyperparameter Transfer Laws for Non-Recurrent Multi-Path Neural Networks

abstract: Deeper modern architectures are costly to tune, and the base learning rate
  is often one of the most sensitive hyperparameters. Maximal Update Parametrization
  ($\mu$P) helps explain why many hyperparameters transfer across width. Yet depthwise
  learning-rate scaling is less understood for modern architectures with convolution,
  residual aggregation, and attention. To unify various non-recurrent multi-path neural
  networks such as CNNs, ResNets, and Transformers, we introduce an architecture-dependent
  notion of effective depth. Under stabilizing initializations and a maximal-update
  criterion, we derive a shared leading-order -3/2 law for the base learning-rate
  scale as effective depth grows. Here, the budget controls typical one-step representation-update
  energy at initialization, and effective depth counts sequential update-bearing units
  while absorbing fixed local structure into constants. Experiments across diverse
  architectures confirm the predicted slope and enable reliable zero-shot transfer
  of learning rates across depths and widths, turning depth scaling into a predictable
  hyperparameter-transfer problem.
