abstract: 'In this paper, we study the role of the critic in actor-critic for entropy-regularized,
  finite, discounted environments. We establish that, when the critic is exact, using
  the latter as a baseline is an actual variance-reduction method. In this case, actor-critic
  with stochastic gradients matches the sample complexity of deterministic policy
  gradient, reaching an $\epsilon$-optimal regularized value with $\tilde{O}(\log(1/\epsilon))$
  samples. In practice, the critic is learned alongside the actor: the variance of
  the actor update is then influenced by the critic’s variance and bias. Specifically,
  when the critic has a sufficiently small error, the variance reduction and rapid
  convergence are preserved. This suggests to learn the critic first, keeping it up
  to date after each actor update, underscoring the pivotal role of accurate critic
  estimation in actor-critic methods.'
